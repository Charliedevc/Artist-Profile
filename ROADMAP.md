# Roadmap

## 1. Foundation

Set up the App Router, strict TypeScript, Tailwind, linting, and dependency lockfile. Verify the local toolchain and document FigCraft access and media-tool gaps.

Seed Narçi’s profile and 26 released records from UnitedMasters. Preserve cover references and verified distribution destinations. Track missing production assets and credits in the README.

## 2. Visual design in FigCraft

Confirm live FigCraft access before beginning final visual design. Define Narçi’s permanent artist shell and establish the visual language with two sample album eras. Prototype the cinematic homepage, discography, dedicated album experience, and persistent player on desktop and mobile. Establish typography, film grain, navigation, transition timing, and reduced-motion behavior.

## 3. Artist platform and catalog

Implement the homepage, discography, album routes, artist biography, contact, and social links. Add the verified streaming destinations and UnitedMasters fan signup. Verify that swapping the featured era preserves older release pages and links. Add upcoming announcements and credits after artist approval.

## 4. Playback and visual media

Add persistent playback across routes, interactive waveforms, and approved unreleased music previews. Secure subscriber audio with server-side entitlement checks and expiring URLs. Add music videos and visualizers. Validate keyboard seeking, mobile playback, load failures, buffering, reduced motion, captions, and media delivery.

## 5. Release readiness

Check responsive behavior, accessibility, page performance, share metadata, and streaming links. Confirm asset approvals and verify the process for launching the next album era.
